    power_offThe breaker keeps tripping

    1. 1

      Unplug the last appliance you connected.

    2. 2

      Reset the breaker once.

    3. 3

      If it trips again, leave it off and call us.

    support_agentNot solved? The duty line can help.

    flash_offThe RCD keeps tripping

    1. 1

      Switch off all breakers and reset the RCD.

    2. 2

      Turn breakers on one by one and note which one trips it.

    3. 3

      Leave that circuit off and request a crew.

    support_agentNot solved? The duty line can help.

    Request a crewarrow_forward

    local_fire_departmentA socket is hot or smells

    1. 1

      Stop using the socket immediately.

    2. 2

      Switch off that circuit's breaker.

    3. 3

      Call the duty line; do not open the socket yourself.

    support_agentNot solved? The duty line can help.

    Request a crewarrow_forward

    lightbulbNo power in part of the house

    1. 1

      Check the panel for a tripped breaker.

    2. 2

      If neighbours are also out, call the distribution company.

    3. 3

      If it is only you, choose "fault" in the quote wizard.

    support_agentNot solved? The duty line can help.

    Request a crewarrow_forward

    electric_meterDoes my panel need replacing?

    1. 1

      If the panel has old screw-in porcelain fuses, it needs replacing.

    2. 2

      If there is no RCD, one must be added.

    3. 3

      Choose "panel replacement" in the wizard and enter the flat area.

    support_agentNot solved? The duty line can help.

    Request a crewarrow_forward

    ev_stationCan an EV charger be installed?

    1. 1

      Read your supply capacity from the bill.

    2. 2

      Measure the distance between the garage or car park and the panel.

    3. 3

      Choose "EV charger" in the wizard and we will book a survey.

    support_agentNot solved? The duty line can help.
